Welcome to Loop

Train your ear the way musicians actually use it: hear a note or chord in a key, then find it by ear and play it back.

  • Play it back. Connect a MIDI keyboard, or use the on-screen keys / your computer keys.
  • Level up. Start with single notes; chords and progressions unlock as you master each step.
  • A few minutes a day. Build a daily streak and your ear gets sharper fast.

Settings

Grading is by scale degree (any octave of the right note counts; exact octave noted). Spaced repetition uses the Leitner system; difficulty targets the 85% rule. Sound uses smplr SplendidGrandPiano (Steinway) samples, with a built-in synth fallback if offline. See BUILD-NOTES.md.
