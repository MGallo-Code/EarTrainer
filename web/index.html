No keyboard? Click the keys, or use computer keys: Z S X D C V G B H N J M (lower octave) and Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U (upper octave).

Grading is by scale degree (any octave of the right note counts; exact octave noted). Spaced repetition uses the Leitner system; difficulty targets the 85% rule. Sound is a built-in synth for this prototype; smplr Steinway samples are the planned upgrade. See BUILD-NOTES.md.
