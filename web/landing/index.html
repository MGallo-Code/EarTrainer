How it works

1

Play it by ear

Work out a bassline or melody on the MIDI keyboard you already own.

2

We clean it up

Your take is captured as exact MIDI and rendered to real piano, tightened so it sounds good.

3

Duet it

Post it as an open challenge. The next player finds the next part by ear.

Who it's for

Adult pop & jazz players who can read music but freeze when asked to just play something by ear. If you want the skill the pros have, Loop drills it the way it's actually used.
