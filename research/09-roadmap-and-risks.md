## Roadmap, Risks & Kill-Criteria

This section assumes the chosen wedge: **Loop** ("Duolingo for playing by ear" with a by-ear duet card), MIDI-first, web-first, hardware in Phase 2. The roadmap deliberately sequences the riskiest unproven bet (the shareable auto-rendered duet clip) as an explicit gate, not a foregone conclusion.

### Phased Roadmap

Each phase has a scope, one primary success metric, and a kill-or-pivot trigger. The phases are ordered so that the cheapest, most-proven engineering ships first and the most capital-intensive bet (hardware) only fires after the viral loop and the niche size are validated.

| Phase | Scope | Primary success metric | Kill / Pivot criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 0 — Drill core MVP (web, Chromium/Android)** | Functional ear-training engine on desktop/Android web only, where Web MIDI fully works (~80% global support, Chrome 43+/Edge 79+/Firefox 108+) [source: https://caniuse.com/midi]. Cadence-primed scale-degree drills (I-IV-V context before each item), MIDI-exact polyphonic grading, Benbassat-style 90%-accuracy mastery gating, smplr SplendidGrandPiano (MIT) for sound [source: https://github.com/danigb/smplr]. Daily streaks. NO duet clip yet. | **First-week streak rate** (Duolingo: first-week streak holders are 3.6x more likely to keep learning [source: https://foundercoho.substack.com/p/inside-duolingos-6b-playbook-gamification]). Target: a streak/retention curve in the range of proven learning apps. | Pivot if D7 retention is structurally below proven learning-app benchmarks AND the cheap ear-training-complaint fixes (adjustable inter-chord gap, audio feedback after errors, relisten, functional drills) don't move it. If the drill core can't retain, the duet loop has nothing to retain. |
| **Phase 1 — The By-Ear Duet Card (the existential bet)** | Build the auto-rendered, MIDI-quantized, shareable duet clip: one user finds a layer by ear, the app aligns/renders it (latency compensation via `AudioContext.outputLatency` [source: https://www.jamieonkeys.dev/posts/web-audio-api-output-latency/]), and posts an open card inviting the next layer. Lean on **original/public-domain loops only** to avoid licensing exposure (Smule's ongoing cover-licensing problem [source: https://www.inc.com/neil-c-hughes/how-this-social-music-app-is-changing-the-rules-of-recording.html]). | **Viral coefficient (K) of the duet card** — does a posted clip pull in the next player? The corpus flags this as the #1, existential risk needing "5-10 rounds of tuning the clip itself." Smule's duet-with-X loop is the proof scale is possible (~20M videos/day; 1M+ duets on one Jessie J post) [source: https://www.inc.com/neil-c-hughes/how-this-social-music-app-is-changing-the-rules-of-recording.html]. | **KILL the social bet, keep the product** if after 5-10 tuning rounds the clip does not generate organic share/invite-accept loops (K well below 1). Pivot to C1/C3 posture: a paid-acquisition by-ear note-checker. This is the single most important gate in the plan — the entire C2 thesis rests here. |
| **Phase 2 — Traction & monetization** | Annual-framed paid tier ($5-8/mo billed annually), web-first Stripe checkout (~97% kept post-Apr 2025 Epic ruling vs Apple's 15-30% [source: https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/apple-anti-steering-ruling-monetization-strategy/]). Bundle 6-12 months free with any future hardware (The ONE pattern [source: https://theonemusic.com/]). Keyboard-manufacturer bundle pitch (3-month free, near-zero CAC — Skoove/Kawai precedent [source: https://kawai.com.au/ca-piano-bonus-offer/]). Teacher/studio auto-graded by-ear homework tier. | **LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1 with CAC payback < 12 months** (standard target [source: https://www.paddle.com/resources/cac-ltv-ratio]); blended CAC trending down as the organic loop carries more of the funnel (Duolingo got to ~80% organic [source: https://www.adexchanger.com/strategy/organic-marketing-is-duolingos-lingua-franca/]). | Pivot pricing/packaging if conversion stays at the freemium floor (~2.18% download-to-paid [source: https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/]) with no hardware-cohort lift. If the $5-8/mo ask can't clear ear-training's free/one-time anchor (Tenuto $4.99 once [source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenuto/id459313476]), harden the paywall or shift weight to the B2B2C studio tier. |
| **Phase 3 — Hardware (only after the loop and niche are validated)** | Lowest-BOM accessory first: a **wired USB-MIDI "duet bar"** to avoid wireless FCC cost (~$1,500+/region [source: https://www.jjrlab.com/news/bluetooth-fcc-certification-cost.html]) and a thin native iOS/iPadOS WebView shell bridging CoreMIDI (the iOS Web MIDI gap is the hardware wedge — Apple has declined Web MIDI since 2020, still absent through Safari 26.5 [source: https://caniuse.com/midi]). Hardware works at basic tier without paying (The ONE/ROLI pattern, NOT Whoop/Snap "brick it" [source: https://roli.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-lumi-membership]). | **Crowdfunding pre-order demand** as the gate (Roadie/Populele proved $100k-$900k+ is achievable [source: https://newatlas.com/music/populele-2-pro-smart-learning-ukulele/]), then hardware-cohort conversion far above the freemium baseline + lower churn (Peloton's device-tethered subs churn 1.2-1.8%/mo vs app-only 8.1%/mo [source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639825/000163982525000077/q32025shareholderletter.htm]). | KILL hardware before any tooling spend if crowdfunding doesn't validate demand. ~9% of funded hardware campaigns fail to deliver, and design + hardware issues each hit >50% of failed campaigns [source: https://dtusciencepark.com/article/26-million-lost-why-crowdfunded-hardware-projects-fail/]. ### Ranked Risk Register

Ranked by a blend of likelihood and severity. R1 is the make-or-break bet; the corpus explicitly names it the #1 risk.

| # | Risk (type) | Why it's real (corpus-grounded) | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **Shareability is unproven (technical + market)** | The auto-rendered MIDI duet clip may simply not be fun to watch the way a singing cover is; if the clip isn't engaging, the viral loop dies and Loop collapses into "just another note-checker." Corpus flags this as #1, needing 5-10 tuning rounds. | Gate it in Phase 1 with a hard K-coefficient kill criterion (above). Quantize MIDI to make every beginner clip look/sound clean — "the product, not the user, makes the clip good." Borrow Smule's proven duet-with-X format [source: https://www.inc.com/neil-c-hughes/how-this-social-music-app-is-changing-the-rules-of-recording.html] rather than inventing a new share unit. |
| **R2** | **Segment-size / niche too small (market)** | "Want to play by ear" may be a passionate-but-narrow niche vs. "play my favorite songs from falling notes" that Simply (~$200M ARR, $1B valuation [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JoyTunes]) dominates. No concept has direct demand evidence. The strongest demand stat the corpus had — adult "returners" 40-65 being ~15% of lessons — is **refuted/unsourced** [source: https://www.artmaster.com/articles/why-more-people-are-learning-in-2025-than-at-any-time-in-decades], so do not lean on it. | The share loop is the hedge: each clip pulls in adjacent keyboard owners who didn't self-identify as "by-ear learners," converting niche-demand risk into top-of-funnel. Target the highest-intent persona (recent digital-keyboard buyers — Skoove bundle conversion proves they convert above the ~2.18% freemium baseline [source: https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/]). Validate niche size cheaply *before* hardware (see top-3 experiments). |
| **R3** | **Incumbent fast-follow (market/execution)** | Simply (GV-backed, $1B [source: https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-joytunes-raising-50m-at-1b-valuation-1001375306]) and Duolingo (the streak engine + a $249 Loog piano [source: https://musically.com/2024/09/25/duolingo-will-soon-be-selling-a-249-piano-for-its-music-course/]) can extend into by-ear. ROLI already launched an AI music coach (Feb 2026) but chose camera/hand-tracking, leaving the audio/by-ear dimension open [source: https://newatlas.com/music/roli-ai-music-coach-piano/]. | Build the data moat fast: a longitudinal dataset of users' own graded by-ear playing trains a by-ear/improv evaluator, an explicitly unsolved problem no recognition-only or mic-based rival has the data for [source: https://www.soundverse.ai/blog/article/ai-practice-tools-for-musicians]. Incumbents' falling-notes/mic-first pipelines are the wrong substrate for a record/layer/duet loop. |
| **R4** | **Engineering surface is the widest of the three (technical)** | C2 bundles the riskiest piece (auto-rendered video + latency-compensated layering) on top of the by-ear engine. Real-time polyphonic mic grading is research-grade: at <30ms tolerance, note-onset F1 collapses to ~31-37% [source: https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07586]. | Stay MIDI-first: MIDI is zero-latency, polyphonic-perfect, and free, sidestepping the entire mic-transcription accuracy/latency triangle. Reuse proven infra: smplr for sound [source: https://github.com/danigb/smplr], in-browser multitrack/overdub is already proven by BandLab/Soundtrap [source: https://jewelmusic.art/blog/web-audio-apis-browser-daws/]. Treat live polyphonic mic grading as R&D, not a v1 promise. |
| **R5** | **Price anchor fights the category (market)** | Ear-training is anchored to free/one-time (Tenuto $4.99 once [source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenuto/id459313476]; free web tools). A $5-8/mo recurring ask may face stiffer resistance than the piano-lessons category where $15-25/mo is normalized (Flowkey $16.99-24.99/mo [source: https://pianistscompass.org/reviews/apps/flowkey/]; Playground $24.99/mo [source: https://www.musicradar.com/reviews/playground-sessions-review]). | Frame price annually ($5-8/mo billed annual ≈ $60-96/yr) — annual retains 44.1% at 1yr vs 17.0% monthly [source: https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/]. Make the by-ear value obvious and fast. Use the hardware buyer as a hard-paywall-equivalent who converts far above the freemium floor. |
| **R6** | **iOS Web MIDI gap excludes a large audience (technical)** | Apple has declined Web MIDI since 2020; it is absent through Safari 26.5, and iOS Safari also suspends Web Audio when backgrounded/locked [source: https://caniuse.com/midi]. The only iOS path is a near-decade-stale third-party browser or your own native shell. | This gap *is* the hardware wedge. Launch desktop/Android-Chromium (~80% global [source: https://caniuse.com/midi]); ship a native iOS/iPadOS WebView shell bridging CoreMIDI in Phase 3 so the same web codebase runs the duet loop on iPad/iPhone. Ship a friendly first-run permission wizard (Chrome 124+ prompts for all MIDI [source: https://developer.chrome.com/blog/web-midi-permission-prompt]) and steer Firefox users (who need a one-time add-on [source: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Mozilla/Firefox/Releases/108]) to Chromium. |

### Top 3 Things to Validate First (cheap experiments)

These are ordered by how decisively they cut the biggest risks (R1, R2, R5) and how cheaply they can run **before** committing to the full duet-clip engineering or any hardware.

**1. Does the auto-rendered duet clip actually spread? (validates R1 — the existential bet)**
- **Experiment:** Before building the full real-time layering engine, hand-produce 10-20 "fake" duet clips from quantized MIDI (Wizard-of-Oz: render manually, no live pipeline) and post them to TikTok/IG with an open "find the next layer by ear" slot. Measure watch-through, shares, and invite-accepts.
- **Why cheap/decisive:** Tests whether a MIDI duet clip is fun to *watch* — the one thing the corpus says is unproven and "existential" — without building the genuinely-unproven auto-render pipeline. Smule's loop scaled on singing (~20M videos/day [source: https://www.inc.com/neil-c-hughes/how-this-social-music-app-is-changing-the-rules-of-recording.html]); this checks whether the MIDI/piano analog carries. If clips don't move, you've saved Phase 1's engineering and can pivot to a paid-acquisition note-checker.

**2. Is the by-ear segment big enough to grow on? (validates R2 — niche-size risk)**
- **Experiment:** Run a smoke-test landing page + Meta/ASA campaigns targeting lookalikes of recent digital-piano purchasers (digital is the entry-level default and growing [source: https://www.sphericalinsights.com/reports/digital-piano-market]) with the "figure out a song by ear on the keyboard you already own" hook. Measure click-through and email/waitlist signup at a known CAC.
- **Why cheap/decisive:** Consumer EdTech is the most cash-efficient acquisition category (~$42 CAC, ~3.8-month payback) so a smoke test is affordable [source: https://www.saas.wtf/p/2024-customer-acquisition-benchmarks]. This directly probes whether "want to play by ear" is a real, reachable audience or a passionate-but-narrow niche — without building the product. Crucially, do NOT rely on the refuted "15% adult returners" stat [source: https://www.artmaster.com/articles/why-more-people-are-learning-in-2025-than-at-any-time-in-decades]; generate first-party demand evidence instead.

**3. Will recent keyboard buyers pay $5-8/mo for by-ear? (validates R5 — price anchor)**
- **Experiment:** A/B a price/paywall test on the Phase 0 drill-core waitlist or early users: $5-8/mo annual-framed vs. a one-time unlock, gated behind the by-ear value. Compare conversion of the recent-keyboard-buyer cohort against the general freemium cohort.
- **Why cheap/decisive:** It tests whether ownership intent overcomes the ear-training free/one-time anchor (Tenuto $4.99 once [source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenuto/id459313476]) before any hardware or pricing infrastructure is built. Freemium converts ~2.18% but hard paywalls ~12.11% [source: https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/], and the hardware buyer is effectively pre-paywalled (Skoove's keyboard-bundle conversion proves the lift [source: https://kawai.com.au/ca-piano-bonus-offer/]). If the high-intent cohort won't clear the anchor, reweight toward the B2B2C studio tier (a channel no incumbent's mic pipeline can grade) before scaling consumer spend.
