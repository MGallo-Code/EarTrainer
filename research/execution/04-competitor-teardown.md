## Competitor Teardown & Positioning

Loop's rivals split into two camps that both leave its core bet uncontested: **play-along piano apps** (Melodics, Simply Piano, Flowkey, Skoove, Yousician) that grade you reproducing notes scrolling down a "note highway," and **ear-training tools** (EarMaster, ToneGym) that train the ear in quiz/game UIs detached from real songs. Neither camp asks you to find notes by ear *as a creative loop*, and not one has a social/duet sharing primitive. Those are universal gaps, not weak spots [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician] [source: https://deviantnoise.net/education/piano/melodics-review/].

### Prioritized Gaps Unmet by ALL Rivals — and How Loop Exploits Each

**1. No one teaches playing by ear as a creative loop (highest-value gap).**
Across all five major learning apps, the practice loop is reading/imitation; by-ear exists only as fragmented single-feature utilities (Functional Ear Trainer, Perfect Ear, Play By Ear), never integrated. The Flowkey deep review "contains no discussion of by-ear training" at all, and the Melodics review literally points users to a *separate* app (HearAndPlay) for it [source: https://pianistscompass.org/reviews/apps/flowkey/] [source: https://deviantnoise.net/education/piano/melodics-review/]. **Exploit:** make cadence-primed, functional (scale-degree) ear grading *inside* a song-building mechanic the headline — it teaches a transferable skill (working out songs yourself) that the falling-bars apps structurally fail to deliver.

**2. No social / collaborative / duet primitive — the entire category is solo.**
None of Simply Piano, Yousician, Flowkey, Skoove, or Melodics lets you record, post, or collaborate on what you played; Yousician deliberately *removed* user-generated content in October 2018 (completed in update 2.58) [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician]. **Exploit:** the open "duet card" (post a layer by ear, invite the next person to add the next layer) is a category-defining mechanic with zero direct competition. This is Loop's wedge and its #1 unproven bet — frame Loop as "the social music app where you build songs by ear together," not "another piano lesson app."

**3. Mic-based detection is acknowledged-broken; MIDI is the fix incumbents tell users to seek.**
Simply Piano's own help center says MIDI "will give you perfect note recognition," yet mic detection remains the default and runs ~25-35ms latent vs ~1-3ms for USB MIDI, and degrades on chords/polyphony [source: https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/6210608-ways-to-improve-note-recognition-in-simply-piano-for-ios-keyboard-electronic-piano] [source: https://the-ultimate-piano.com/learn/optimizing-audio-latency/]. (Caveat from the verified record: rivals like Piano Marvel already do reliable 2-3 note mic detection, so frame this as "zero-latency, polyphonic MIDI-exact" rather than "rivals can't hear chords at all.") **Exploit:** make zero-latency, polyphonic, MIDI-exact grading a demoable headline for the pop/jazz adult who plays full voicings — the one claim every incumbent's own docs validate.

**4. "Illusion of progress" — users master a proprietary interface, not music.**
Simply Piano/JoyTunes (valued ~$1B+, ~$200M annual revenue, 10M+ downloads, ~$170/yr, retention 2.5x edtech average) produces advanced users who are "functionally illiterate" at sheet music and stuck in the "Three-Chord Wonder Trap," unable to improvise or play without the app [source: https://divinations.substack.com/p/simply-pianos-silent-symphony]. **Exploit:** target "illusion-of-progress refugees" directly in ad copy and Reddit/TikTok seeding — people who "finished" Simply Piano/Yousician but still can't play without it.

**5. Auto-renewal billing is the loudest churn driver.**
Yousician sits at 1.2/5 on PissedConsumer (100% unfavorable), with documented charges of $139.99 twice and ~$300; Simply Piano shows EUR 134.99 forgotten-cancel charges [source: https://yousician.pissedconsumer.com/review.html]. (Verified correction: Melodics is actually *praised* for renewal reminders and a 14-day refund window — the "GBP 131.88 overcharge" was a mislabel of its USD annual list price — so concentrate this attack on Yousician and Simply Piano, not "all incumbents") [source: https://melodics.com/plans]. **Exploit:** weaponize transparent billing — no card for the free loop, an explicit pre-renewal email, one-tap in-app cancel, and a "we'll never silently auto-charge you" pledge on the pricing page.

**6. Binary pass/fail grading lets you advance playing wrong rhythms/durations.**
Flowkey moves you on "whether you play... without a single mistake or ten wrong notes" and has no scoring system; Skoove lets you release notes early and still earn a perfect score [source: https://pianistscompass.org/reviews/apps/flowkey/] [source: https://pianistscompass.org/reviews/apps/skoove/]. **Exploit:** Loop's MIDI-exact note+timing+polyphony grading is genuinely stricter and more honest — but pair it with cadence priming so it reads as scaffolding, not a failure wall.

**7. Content vanishes and runs out; free tiers gate the wrong thing.**
Yousician users report favorited songs disappearing 7-8 months in (licensing churn); Flowkey has only 2 advanced courses and is completable in ~1 year; Skoove's free tier is 3 lessons with a card-required trial [source: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/yousician.com] [source: https://pianistscompass.org/reviews/apps/flowkey/] [source: https://pianistscompass.org/reviews/apps/skoove/]. **Exploit:** a community-created (UGC) duet library is permanent and ever-growing, neutralizing both the "songs vanished" and "ran out of content" complaints. Critically — **do NOT gate the shareable clip** (it's the acquisition engine); gate on duet creations/day or advanced grading modes instead, copying the *form* of Yousician's 15-min cap or EarMaster's 2-exercise free tier without throttling virality [source: https://www.guitarchalk.com/yousician-cost/].

### Crisp Positioning vs Each Rival

- **vs Melodics** (nearest UX rival): "Melodics shows you the notes; Loop makes you find them by ear, then posts the result." Melodics owns satisfying low-latency MIDI grading on a note highway and is MIDI-native — but has no by-ear, no songs-by-ear, no theory depth, and no social, so even the closest MIDI competitor leaves Loop's exact niche uncontested. Win on (a) by-ear discovery and (b) the shareable duet artifact, not on grading polish [source: https://deviantnoise.net/education/piano/melodics-review/].

- **vs Simply Piano**: "Stop watching red/green. Play full chords and get zero-latency, MIDI-exact feedback — then share it." Attack the mic-default pass/fail UX and the illusion-of-progress trap; mirror their single-paid-tier + aggressive annual discount pricing simplicity [source: https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/collections/1512479-simply-piano-note-recognition-and-midi-help] [source: https://www.alibaba.com/product-insights/how-much-is-simply-piano-in-2026-full-pricing-breakdown.html].

- **vs Yousician**: "All the streaks and leaderboards, plus the social loop they deleted." Yousician owns the gamified funnel but removed UGC in 2018 and is investing R&D in AI Vocal Coach / Kids, not collaboration — the duet lane is wide open [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician] [source: https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-review/].

- **vs EarMaster**: "Functional ear training that ends in a song you can post, not a decontextualized quiz." Borrow their validated cadence-primed scale-degree science; differentiate on real-song context, MIDI-keyboard performance capture, and a shareable artifact [source: https://www.earmaster.com/products/ear-training-sight-singing/version-comparison.html].

- **vs ToneGym** (the one rival NOT to dramatically undercut on price): "ToneGym is a gym for your ears; Loop is the song you build with someone else." At ~$75/yr, ToneGym's pricing is competitive with Loop's $5-8/mo annual ($60-96/yr), so differentiate on the duet/by-ear product and the network effect their 400k solo users lack — not on price [source: https://www.tonegym.co/shop/index] [source: https://www.tonegym.co/].

### Pricing Posture

Loop's planned **$5-8/mo billed annually ($60-96/yr) undercuts every paid play-along tier**: Melodics (~$120-180/yr), Simply Piano ($119.99-169.90/yr), and Yousician Premium+ ($139.99/yr) [source: https://melodics.com/plans] [source: https://www.alibaba.com/product-insights/how-much-is-simply-piano-in-2026-full-pricing-breakdown.html] [source: https://www.guitarchalk.com/yousician-cost/]. Lead with the annual price and a ~50-60% vs-monthly discount, copying Simply Piano's single-paid-tier simplicity. ToneGym (~$75/yr) is the sole exception where Loop competes on product, not price.

### Two Real Competitive Risks

1. **Fast-follow.** Melodics and Yousician already capture MIDI/audio and have the engineering to bolt on a share/duet layer if Loop validates the mechanic. Speed to a defensible UGC network effect — and a recognizable, consistent clip aesthetic (smplr piano render) that the format becomes associated with — matters more than the underlying tech [source: https://deviantnoise.net/education/piano/melodics-review/].

2. **The clip itself is unproven.** No competitor ships an auto-rendered MIDI duet clip, so this teardown de-risks *positioning* but not the existential question of whether that clip is fun to watch/share. The Wizard-of-Oz test, not this teardown, is the real validation — and Loop's Web MIDI dependency (desktop/Android, not iOS Safari) creates a capture-vs-distribution split against iOS-heavy TikTok/IG until the phase-2 iOS shell ships [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician].
